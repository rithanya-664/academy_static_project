Welcome to Rhythm Academy

Founded with a passion for artistic purity, Rhythm Academy of Music & Dance is Chennai's premier destination for nurturing creative talent. Located in the heart of Perungudi, we bridge the gap between classical heritage and contemporary innovation.

We believe that music and dance are not just arts to be memorized, but a lifestyle that builds discipline, grace, and confidence. Whether you are stepping onto the dance floor for the first time or training to master an instrument, our certified instructors provide personalized guidance to help you find your unique rhythm and thrive both on stage and in life.



Why Choose Rhythm?

Expert Mentorship: Learn from certified professionals with years of industry and stage experience.

Diverse Curriculum: From traditional Carnatic vocals and Bharatanatyam to Western pop and Hip-Hop.

Flexible Formats: Structured courses designed carefully for kids, working professionals, and advanced learners.

Performance Opportunities: Regular recitals, stage shows, and event showcases to build real-world confidence.
